# Success Metrics

## Purpose

This document defines how the business and delivery team will judge whether the platform is achieving its intended outcomes.

## Commercial Metrics

| ID | Metric | Definition | Initial Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| MET-COM-001 | Gross merchandise value | Value of completed sales before refunds | Establish baseline |
| MET-COM-002 | Net sales | Completed sales after discounts and refunds | Establish baseline |
| MET-COM-003 | Conversion rate | Completed orders divided by eligible sessions | Improve over time |
| MET-COM-004 | Average order value | Net sales divided by completed orders | Establish baseline |
| MET-COM-005 | Repeat customer rate | Customers with more than one completed order | Establish baseline |
| MET-COM-006 | Cart abandonment rate | Started carts not converted within period | Reduce over time |
