# Policies and Operating Assumptions

## Purpose

This document captures provisional business policies so design work can continue before final legal and commercial wording is supplied.

These are not final legal terms.

## Pricing Policy

Initial assumption:

- All customer-facing prices are displayed in ZAR.
- Prices are displayed inclusive of VAT only if the business is VAT-registered and legally required to do so.
- The payable total is confirmed before payment.
- The order retains a snapshot of prices used at purchase time.
- Pricing errors may require controlled cancellation and refund handling.

## Delivery Policy

Initial assumption:

- Delivery is available only to supported South African addresses.
- Delivery fees are calculated or selected during checkout.
- Estimated delivery time is presented as an estimate, not a guarantee.
- Delivery time starts after payment confirmation and fulfilment processing.
- Risk and ownership wording must be reviewed in final terms.
- Collection is disabled until specifically approved.

## Returns and Exchanges Policy

Initial assumption:

- Customers may request a return or exchange within a business-defined period.
- Items must meet condition and packaging requirements.
- Certain items may be excluded where lawful.
- Return shipping responsibility depends on the reason for return.
- Refunds are processed only after approval and required inspection.
- The initial process may be support-assisted rather than fully automated.

## Refund Policy

Initial assumption:

- Refunds require authorised review.
- Refunds are made through the original payment route where supported.
- Refund status is recorded against payment and order records.
- Partial refunds may be introduced if supported by the selected provider.
- Refund actions are audited.

## Privacy Policy Direction

The platform will explain:

- What information is collected
- Why it is collected
- How it is used
- Which providers receive it
- How long it is retained
- Customer rights and contact mechanisms
- Cookie and analytics use
- Marketing consent management

## Account Policy

Initial assumption:

- Guest checkout is supported.
- Account registration is optional.
- Registered customers can access order history and saved addresses.
- Suspicious or abusive accounts may be restricted.
- Password reset uses a time-limited secure token.

## Promotion Policy

Initial assumption:

- Voucher eligibility is validated at checkout.
- Vouchers cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Promotions cannot reduce the payable total below zero.
- Stacking is disabled by default.
- Usage may be limited per customer or globally.

## Customer Support Policy

Initial assumption:

- Support is available through email and optionally WhatsApp.
- Order number is the preferred support reference.
- Identity verification is required before sensitive details or changes are disclosed.
- Support interactions affecting orders should be recorded.
