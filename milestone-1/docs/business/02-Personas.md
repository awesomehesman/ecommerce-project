# User Personas

## Purpose

Personas provide a practical design reference for the people who will use or operate the platform. They are working assumptions and must be refined using actual customer and business data after launch.

---

## Persona 1 — Mobile-First Streetwear Shopper

### Profile

- Age range: 18–34
- Location: Urban or peri-urban South Africa
- Device: Primarily smartphone
- Digital behaviour: Social media, mobile banking, online shopping
- Shopping motivation: New drops, exclusivity, style, brand identity

### Goals

- Discover new products quickly
- See accurate size and colour availability
- Understand price and delivery cost
- Complete checkout with minimal friction
- Receive proof that the order was successful
- Track order progress

### Frustrations

- Slow image-heavy websites
- Hidden delivery fees
- Unclear sizing
- Products showing as available when they are not
- Forced account creation
- Payment uncertainty
- Poor mobile navigation

### Design Implications

- Mobile-first layout
- Fast product imagery
- Clear size selector
- Prominent stock status
- Guest checkout
- Familiar payment methods
- Immediate confirmation
- Simple order-status presentation

## Persona 2 — Returning Loyal Customer

### Profile

- Has purchased previously
- Recognises the brand and product fit
- May follow campaigns and releases
- Values speed and convenience

### Goals

- Reuse saved delivery information
- Review previous purchases
- Reorder quickly
- Save products to a wishlist
- Receive relevant launch or promotion information

### Frustrations

- Re-entering data
- Forgotten passwords
- Poor order history
- Irrelevant marketing
- No visibility into saved items

### Design Implications

- Secure account access
- Address book
- Order history
- Wishlist
- Password reset
- Preference management
- Fast repeat checkout

---

## Persona 3 — Gift Buyer

### Profile

- May not know the brand or sizing well
- Shopping for another person
- Needs confidence and guidance

### Goals

- Understand categories and size guidance
- Find popular or featured items
- Know delivery timelines
- Understand returns and exchanges

### Frustrations

- Brand-specific language without explanation
- Incomplete product details
- Unclear exchange rules
- No delivery estimate

### Design Implications

- Clear product copy
- Size guide
- Popular or featured collections
- Visible delivery and returns information
- Support contact options

---

## Persona 4 — Store Administrator

### Profile

- Responsible for daily e-commerce operations
- May not be highly technical
- Handles products, stock, orders, and content

### Goals

- Add and update products quickly
- Correct stock and pricing
- Publish campaigns
- Identify paid orders requiring action
- Update fulfilment statuses
- Find customer and order information

### Frustrations

- Complex admin forms
- Unclear order states
- Accidental publication of incomplete products
- Manual duplicate work
- No low-stock visibility

### Design Implications

- Guided forms
- Draft and publish states
- Validation
- Searchable order list
- Low-stock alerts
- Audit trail
- Role-based permissions

---

## Persona 5 — Customer Support Agent

### Profile

- Responds to order, delivery, payment, and product queries
- Requires fast access to accurate data

### Goals

- Find an order using order number, email, or customer details
- Understand order and payment state
- Review communication history
- Escalate refund or fulfilment issues

### Frustrations

- Conflicting systems
- Missing payment information
- No audit history
- Inability to explain order status

### Design Implications

- Unified order view
- Payment status
- Timeline of status changes
- Customer contact details
- Notes and audit records
- Restricted support permissions

---

## Persona 6 — Business Owner or Manager

### Profile

- Responsible for commercial results and operational health
- Uses summaries rather than transaction-level detail

### Goals

- Understand sales performance
- Identify popular products
- Review stock risks
- Monitor order backlog
- Understand payment and refund trends

### Frustrations

- Spreadsheet consolidation
- Delayed reporting
- Inconsistent definitions
- No trusted source of truth

### Design Implications

- KPI dashboard
- Date filters
- Export capability
- Clear metric definitions
- Role-restricted financial information
